THE PHANEROZOIC

538.8 MILLION YEARS AGO–NOW

This is the eon of abundant fossil life: shells, forests, dinosaurs, flowering plants, mammals, and every living ecosystem we know today.

◆ABOUT THE PHANEROZOIC

The Phanerozoic begins with the Cambrian Period, when many animal groups gained hard parts that fossilize well. Its three eras—Paleozoic, Mesozoic, and Cenozoic—hold dramatic radiations and mass extinctions, including the rise and fall of non-avian dinosaurs and the later expansion of mammals.

THREE BIG ERAS

Paleozoic, Mesozoic, and Cenozoic divide the eon’s fossil-rich history.

Shells and bones fossilize more readily, making this eon appear especially vivid.

NOT A STRAIGHT LINE
