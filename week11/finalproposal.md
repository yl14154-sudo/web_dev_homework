
# AUFXKOO – E-commerce Website Proposal

## Project Overview

For my final project, I plan to build an e-commerce website for a fictional home and kitchenware brand called **AUFXKOO**. The website will sell furniture, ceramic cups, plates, and kitchen accessories.

The design will follow a **Scandinavian Minimalist** style, focusing on clean layouts, simple typography, natural product images, and a modern shopping experience.

## Design Inspiration

- [HAY](https://www.hay.com/) – Modern product layouts and clean design.
- [Ferm Living](https://fermliving.com/pages/newsletter-page) – Scandinavian style and lifestyle photography.

I will use these websites as inspiration while creating my own brand identity and website layout.

## Website Features

- Homepage: Brand introduction and featured products.
- Shop Page: Product listings and category filters.
- Product Details: Product images, descriptions, and prices.
- Shopping Cart: Add, remove, and update products.
- About Us: Brand story and introduction.
- Responsive Design: Support desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
